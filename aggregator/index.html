Executive Summary

The Vision

Not a marketplace. Not a middleman. The operational backbone of Iraq's food supply chain — connecting every farmer, every restaurant, every city through one trusted, intelligent platform that ensures nothing goes to waste and everyone gets a fair deal.

The Problem

$400M+ lost annually in Baghdad alone from spoilage, price manipulation, and supply chain waste.

Price Opacity

No way to compare or verify market prices. Middlemen control everything.

Supply Chaos

No advance ordering. Frequent shortages or surplus waste. 20-30% value lost.

Cash Only

All transactions cash-based at 2 AM markets. Security risks, no traceability.

No Quality Control

Variable quality, no standardized grading, no recourse for buyers.

Sellers Exploited

Small farmers have no leverage. Big sellers monopolize pricing.

No Logistics

No tracking, no coordination, delivery delays, post-harvest losses.

The Solution

One platform that accepts, grades, processes, stores, and distributes — end to end.

Accept & Grade

Quality check every product. Grade A, B, C. Route by grade.

Process & Package

Clean, cut, can, freeze, package. Ready-to-use or export-ready.

Funnel & Distribute

Surplus in one city fills shortage in another. AI-driven logistics.

Export

Cut, freeze, package for international markets. Iraq becomes an exporter.
