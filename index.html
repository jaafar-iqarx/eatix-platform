Behavioral Foundation

The Psychology Behind EatiX

For behaviour to change, the software must trigger three psychological drivers.

Immediate Reward

Every meaningful action triggers accomplishment — completing an order, earning points, closing a shift. The software rewards instantly with visual feedback and progress indicators. No action goes unnoticed.

Social Incentives

Humans are competitive. EatiX uses comparison — loyalty leaderboards, cashier rankings, branch comparisons, popularity badges. Our innate desire to measure up drives engagement.

Progress Monitoring

Always show trajectory: up, down, or stable. Tier progress bars, points-to-next-reward, food cost trends, shift performance. Never leave the user wondering where they stand.
